What Does a Pilot Do?

A pilot is responsible for operating an aircraft and helping ensure that every flight is conducted safely and according to aviation procedures.

  • ✦ Plans and prepares for flights
  • ✦ Operates and monitors aircraft systems
  • ✦ Communicates with Air Traffic Control
  • ✦ Monitors weather and flight conditions
  • ✦ Follows aviation rules and procedures
  • ✦ Works closely with the flight crew
👨‍✈️

A Pilot's Mindset

Aviation requires discipline, communication, technical knowledge, decision-making and continuous learning.


Pilots do not simply "fly a plane". They manage a complex system while working with other aviation professionals.
